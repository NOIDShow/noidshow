About the show

No ads.No spin.No ID.

NO ID is Idaho's independent, producer-supported podcast that deconstructs the mainstream media.

Every week, Paul and Scott go through the headlines, press releases and local TV packages coming out of Boise, Coeur d'Alene, Post Falls and Spokane. They ask who's framing the story, what got left out, and who benefits.

It's hyper-local, often funny, and never sponsored. The show runs entirely on support from listeners like you.
