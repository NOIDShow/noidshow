About the show

No ads.No spin.NO ID.

NO ID is Idaho's independent, value-4-value podcast that deconstructs the mainstream media.

Every week, Paul and Scott go through the headlines, press releases, and local TV "news" packages coming out of Boise, Coeur d'Alene, and everywhere in between. Who is framing the story? What got left out? Who benefits?

It's hyper-local, often humorous, and never sponsored. The show runs entirely on support from producers like you.
